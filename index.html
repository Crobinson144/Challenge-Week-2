About Me

I'm Chinua Eric Robinson, a data analyst focused on turning raw data into decisions people can act on. I worked my way from entry-level jobs to leadership roles in security, finance and data analytics, and I hold a BA in Psychology and a Master's in Industrial and Organizational Psychology, so I approach data with the people and processes behind it in mind. I work in Excel, SQL, Tableau, Python and R, and completed Johns Hopkins data science coursework in R. I'm also the author of Your Children's Children, a book about building generational wealth. This page collects the front-end projects I built while learning HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
